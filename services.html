What we provide

Our team at With Grace works relentlessly to ensure we provide a flawless service. Our organisation runs weekly markets throughout December, to ensure the produce used is at its peak quality. Many of our volunteers are locals from the community, driven by hope, they work alongside our team in order to provide high-quality comforting meals. Through simple acts of care, With Grace strengthens the community and makes sure no one feels left behind.

About Our Suppliers


With Grace

Our menu is built on the exceptional ingredients sourced from trusted Northern Irish producers, each bringing their own craft to our kitchen. We work closely with Season Harvest, whose vibrant vegetables—pulled from rich local soil—arrive to us at peak freshness. Our meats come from Lisdergan’s Butchers, renowned for their grass‑fed beef and lamb, raised with care on small farms that prioritise quality and flavour. From the coast, we proudly serve fish supplied by Donegal Prime, whose dedication to sustainable, cold‑water sourcing ensures every fillet carries the clean taste of the Atlantic. Together, these partnerships allow us to celebrate the very best of our region on every plate.

We ensure that the ingredients we use are the highest quality attainable.

Our Headquarters

Our headquarters is based in the heart of Derry, North of Ireland; where we manage this nation wide operation. With Grace's headquarters has pulled the community of Derry together, essentially becoming a pillar for what the community stands for: a refuge in hard times, a stage for local voices, a living symbol of Derry's spirit.

Jobs

Everyone helps where they're needed most. Some cook warm meals, others serve with a smile, and a few gather donated ingredients from local shops. There are people who wash dishes, people who sit and chat with guests, and people who quietly make sure no one goes hungry. Every job is an act of kindness, not a title, and everyone works side by side with the same purpose: to feed the homeless with dignity and compassion.

Do volunteers need experience?

No, volunteers do not need any previous experience. We provide all necessary training and support to ensure everyone can contribute effectively.

How often are meals served?

Meals are served regularly throughout everyday of the week. The schedule is flexible and can cater to anyone.

Do you accept donations?

Yes, we accepts various donations which can very from ingredients, supplies or even monetary donations, which are all welcome.

Who can come for a meal

Anyone who's in need of one. No questions asked, no judgements.
