About Us

With Grace is a volunteer-led catering team based in the heart of Northern Ireland, founded by culinary scholar Royce Roque Arnaiz in 2019. Since then, the nature of the company has captivated thousands of volunteers around the nation, all united by a shared mission to defeat poverty and hunger, and to also spread love and goodwill. With Grace stands as a testament to the power of kindness: a small, independent catering effort that transforms cold winter nights into moments of warmth and humanity for Northern Ireland’s homeless community.

Our Mission

At With Grace, our mission is to bring warmth, dignity, and nourishment to those experiencing homelessness during the harsh winter months. We believe that a hot meal is more than just food - it is comfort, compassion, and a reminder that nobody is alone. Through the generosity of thousands of donors and our catering efforts, we strive to create communities within Northern Ireland that embody love and care, ensuring every plate that's served carries humanity and hope for a brighter future.
